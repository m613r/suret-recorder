I'm learning Suret and want to learn it in your voice, the way your family says it. Each card shows an English word or phrase. Record how you'd say it, and it goes straight into my flashcards.

How to record

  • Tap the red button, say the word once, clearly, then tap again to stop.
  • Tap Play to check it. Redo if you want another try.
  • If you want, type how it's written: English letters or Syriac script are both fine. Then tap Save & next.
  • Not sure, or your family doesn't use that word? Tap Skip.

A few notes

  • "how Madison would say it" means say it as a woman would say it about herself.
  • "say both" means record both versions back to back: talking to a man, then to a woman.
  • Do as many as you want at a time. It remembers where you left off.
  • If it opened inside Discord and the mic won't work, open the link in Safari or Chrome instead.
